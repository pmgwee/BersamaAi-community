## 📢 Help us tune your AI feed — react to the news!

You'll now see **three reactions** on every news post across our channels. One tap is all it takes — and it directly shapes what we post next.

- 👍 **Useful** — glad I saw this
- 🔥 **Fire** — this is amazing / a must-see
- 👎 **Not for me** — not really my thing (no judgment at all — it just helps us learn)

**Why we're doing this:** BersamaAi learns from what you react to. The more you tap, the better our feed gets at surfacing AI news you actually care about — and less of what you don't. Think of it as **training our community's own AI feed, together** 🤝

💬 **Replies count too** — if a post sparks a thought, hit *Reply* and share it. Thoughtful replies carry even more weight than a reaction.

It takes a second, it's honest, and it makes BersamaAi better for everyone. Thanks for being here — now go react! 🚀

### Owner notes (don't post)
- The reactions are seeded by the bot (it adds 👍🔥👎 to every news card so members have something to click). The sweep **subtracts the bot's own tap** — only **member** reactions count toward the reward.
- Reward weights: 👍 +2, 🔥 +3, 👎 −2, any other emoji +1, a reply +4 — normalized by active-member count, clamped to [−3, +5].
- Members can also add any OTHER emoji (🎉❤️🤯…) — each counts as mild positive engagement (+1).
- The feed will NOT become an echo chamber: a genuinely viral item still gets posted even if its category is "disliked." Reactions only break ties between similarly-hot items.
